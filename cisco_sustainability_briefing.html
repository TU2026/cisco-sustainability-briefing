Water Stewardship

WRI Launches Water Scopes 1-3 Framework — $77B Corporate Value Exposed to Supply Chain Water Risk

The World Resources Institute (WRI), with partners SCS Global Services, Pacific Institute, CEO Water Mandate, and WWF, published the Water Scopes 1-3 technical framework on April 30, 2026. This standard — modeled after the GHG Protocol — provides the first unified methodology for companies to measure and disclose water risk across their full value chains (direct operations → upstream supply chain → downstream product use). Key finding: companies that extend water risk assessment into supply chains identify supply chain water risks at 7× the rate of companies measuring only direct operations. CDP estimates $77 billion in global corporate value is exposed to supply chain water risk. The framework aligns with SBTN, TNFD, and EU CSRD disclosure requirements.
